=====================================
SIGNAL SPECIFICATIONS
=====================================
Framework: 5 signals + composite MasterScore + multiple testing discipline
References: Kyle (1985), Pan & Poteshman (2006), Jeng/Metrick/Zeckhauser (2003),
            Giannone/Reichlin/Small (2008), Agarwalla/Jacob/Varma (2014), Harvey/Liu/Zhu (2016),
            Ball & Brown (1968), Bernard & Thomas (1989), Novy-Marx (2015)

--- Signal 1: Pre-Earnings Delivery Conviction (CADP) ---
Theory: Kyle (1985) informed trading model. Informed traders must take delivery (can't
        square off intraday), so delivery % is a noisy proxy for informed order flow.
Construction:
  ADP_{i,t} = DP_{i,t} - mean(DP_{i, [-60,-11]})     # abnormal delivery %
  CADP_i = sum(ADP_{i,t}) for t in [-10, -1]           # cumulative over pre-earnings window
Coverage: 1,967 symbols

--- Signal 2: F&O Informed Positioning ---
Theory: Pan & Poteshman (2006) — option order flow predicts stock returns.
Construction:
  a) OI-Price regime classification per stock-day (4 states: long_buildup, short_buildup, short_covering, long_unwinding)
  b) PCR_OI = put_OI / call_OI (contrarian at extremes)
  c) FII_bias vs Retail_bias divergence (aggregate level)
  Composite: OI regime + PCR z-score + OI momentum
Coverage: 282 F&O symbols

--- Signal 3: Corporate Health Index (CHI) ---
Theory: Jeng/Metrick/Zeckhauser (2003) — insider buying predicts returns, selling doesn't.
Construction:
  a) delta_PS = promoter_pct(q) - promoter_pct(q-1)
  b) InsiderNetBuy = sum(acquisition_value) - sum(disposal_value), trailing 90 days
  c) PledgeActivity = pledge_count - revoke_count, trailing 90 days
  CHI = weighted composite, buying weighted more than selling (asymmetric)
Coverage: 339 symbols

--- Signal 4: Macro Sector Revenue Nowcaster ---
Theory: Giannone/Reichlin/Small (2008) nowcasting framework.
Construction:
  Composite of GST YoY + IIP YoY + Credit Growth YoY
  12-month trailing z-score for regime detection
  Sector momentum from IIP sub-indices
Coverage: Market-wide (uniform tilt applied to all stocks)

--- Signal 5: Standardized Unexpected Earnings (SUE) ---
Theory: Ball & Brown (1968), Bernard & Thomas (1989) — PEAD.
Construction:
  SUE_i = (EPS_q - EPS_{q-4}) / std(sequential EPS changes, trailing 8 quarters)
  FM (Novy-Marx proxy) = avg(EPS, last 4 qtrs) - avg(EPS, prev 4 qtrs)
Data source: screener.in quarterly EPS (1,142 symbols with data, 1,048 with 8+ quarters)
Coverage: 1,048 symbols

--- Signal 6: MasterScore (Composite) ---
Theory: DeMiguel et al. (2009) — equal-weight often beats optimized weights.
Construction:
  MasterScore_i = mean(z-score of each available signal) + 0.1 * macro_z
  Components: CADP, F&O, CHI, SUE, Momentum, Value+Quality (+ macro tilt). Smart-Money excluded (sparse).
  Minimum 2 signals required. Cross-sectional z-score per signal.
Coverage: 1,440 stocks with 2+ signals; 169 with full 6-signal coverage

--- Signal 7: Price Momentum ---
Theory: Jegadeesh & Titman (1993), Novy-Marx (2012), George & Hwang (2004, 52w-high).
Construction:
  ret_12_1 = close[t-21]/close[t-252] - 1   # skip last month to avoid 1-mo reversal
  ret_6_1  = close[t-21]/close[t-126] - 1
  pct_52w_high = close[t] / max(close, trailing 252d)
  mom_score = 0.6*z(ret_12_1) + 0.4*z(pct_52w_high), winsorized ±3σ; 200DMA = trend flag
  Liquidity gate: avg 20d turnover ≥ ₹1cr (turnover stored in LAKH units)
  Gap handling: anchors at the last continuous ≥252-day block (equity_daily has a 367-day hole)
Coverage: 1,244 liquid stocks, as-of anchor date (currently 2025-05-16 until backfill)

--- Signal 8: Value + Quality ---
Theory: Fama-French value (HML), Novy-Marx (2013) quality/profitability, Piotroski (2000, simplified).
Construction:
  Value  = mean z of [earnings_yield=1/PE (PE>0), book_to_price=BV/Price, dividend_yield]
  Quality= mean z of [ROE, ROCE, -debt_to_equity, OPM_trend (last4-prev4 avg), profit_consistency (frac of 8q +ve)]
  vq_score = 0.5*Value + 0.5*Quality (each winsorized ±3σ)
